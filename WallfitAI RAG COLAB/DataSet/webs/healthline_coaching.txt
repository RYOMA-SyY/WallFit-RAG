========================================
WOMEN'S FITNESS & COACHING INSIGHTS
========================================

RECOVERY & REST
------------------------------------
- Olympic track star Allyson Felix emphasizes that recovery is a core pillar of elite performance.
- Stress directly disrupts sleep quality — coaches should address stress management as part of recovery planning.
- Better sleep = better training adaptation; poor sleep undermines results regardless of program quality.

GLP-1 MEDICATIONS & SUPPLEMENTATION
------------------------------------
- Women taking GLP-1s (e.g., Ozempic) may require additional supplementation to avoid nutrient deficiencies.
- Coaches working with clients on GLP-1s should factor in potential changes in appetite, muscle mass, and energy levels.

STRESS MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------
- Vagus nerve stimulators are gaining traction as a stress-relief tool — reviewed positively.
- Chronic stress impacts sleep, recovery, digestion, and hormonal balance — all critical factors in female fitness.

NUTRITION & WEIGHT MANAGEMENT
------------------------------------
- Macronutrient tracking (carbs, protein, fat) is a practical tool for coaches to guide client nutrition.
- Calorie awareness supports weight management goals when aligned with individual needs.
- Protein and fiber remain key nutritional priorities.
- Prebiotics in 2026 are expert-recommended for gut health support.

SKIN & SENSITIVE HEALTH CONSIDERATIONS
------------------------------------
- Fabric choice matters for women with sensitive skin during workouts — coaches can advise on appropriate activewear.
- Sunscreen and skin protection are part of holistic health for active women training outdoors.

DIGESTIVE & GUT HEALTH
------------------------------------
- Specific exercises can help relieve constipation — relevant for clients with digestive discomfort affecting training.
- Diet and prebiotics play a role in gut health optimization.

MENTAL WELL-BEING
------------------------------------
- Anxiety, depression, and stress are health topics directly linked to training outcomes.
- Coaches should be aware of mental health signals and refer clients to professionals when needed.
